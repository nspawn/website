## Chat

The team and other users are in `#nspawn-org` on
[Matrix](https://matrix.to/#/#nspawn-org:matrix.org) and in `#nspawn-org` on
[Libera.Chat](https://web.libera.chat/#nspawn-org) (IRC). Questions, ideas
and "does this work on my distribution" all fit there.

## Reporting

Bug reports go to the issues of the repository they belong to:
[nspawn](https://github.com/nspawn/nspawn/issues) for the tool,
[mkosi-definitions](https://github.com/nspawn/mkosi-definitions/issues) for
the images on the hub, and [nspawn.org](https://github.com/nspawn/nspawn.org/issues)
for this site. When you report a problem with a machine, the output of
`nspawn ps`, `nspawn logs NAME --all` and `systemctl --version` usually tells
most of the story.

The [code of conduct](https://github.com/nspawn/coc) of the nspawn
organization applies to every repository, to the chat channels and to the
mailing address.
